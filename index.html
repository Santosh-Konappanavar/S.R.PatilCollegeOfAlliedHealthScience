About the College

Excellence in Allied Health Education

A premier institution for healthcare training

S R Patil College of Allied Health Science is dedicated to providing comprehensive education in healthcare and allied health sciences. Our mission is to produce skilled, compassionate, and professional healthcare workers who can make a meaningful contribution to society.

With modern infrastructure, experienced faculty, and a commitment to holistic development, we prepare students for successful careers in the dynamic healthcare industry. Every program combines theoretical knowledge with hands-on skills training so graduates arrive on the job ready to work.

The college is part of the S R Patil Education Foundation, Badagandi, which has been building rural access to quality education in Bilagi Taluk since 2010.

A Message from Our Founder

S R Patil College of Allied Health Science was built on a simple belief: talent in rural Karnataka deserves the same quality of opportunity as anywhere else. Our founder established the S R Patil Education Foundation in Badagandi to bring exactly that — modern, industry-ready education, close to home.

Every program on this campus is designed around one outcome: graduates who walk into a hospital or clinic already knowing how to do the work.

Hon.S R Patil
